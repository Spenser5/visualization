**What software did you use to create your data visualization?**
Excel

**Who is your intended audience?**
People running, planning, or funding registered programs in Toronto
    
**What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization?**
I am trying to convey the amount of people registering for programs in toronto each year, while also comparing the registration numbers for each season within each year. This shows if there is growth/declining participation in registered programs each year and also which seasons are most popular. 

    
**What aspects of design did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply them? With what elements of your plots?**

I think the plot is aesthetically pleasing with 4 coloured trendlines and a white background with a faded grid so it is easier to see which point lines up with which number. I think it is simple and not overcrowded but conveys the message it is supposed to. The figure is substantive as it accurately shows the data with one differently coloured line for each season. One issue is that there was no data for fall of 2015, but i chose to include 2015 for the other seasons as it is still usefull to compare amongst them. The plot is also perceptive as it is simple enough to show a clear message of registered program enrollement over time. 
    
**How did you ensure that your data visualizations are reproducible? If the tool you used to make your data visualization is not reproducible, how will this impact your data visualization?** 
The data is open source and I simply used excel's charting function to create the figure so it should be fully reproducible by others.

**How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible?**  
I changed some of the colors excel automatically used to try and make the 4 lines easliy distinguishable from eachother.
    
**Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization?**
People planning and funding the registered programs could be impacted by this figure as they could use it to decide whether programs need more promoting or improvements based on enrollment. People of Toronto planning on registering for future programs could be impacted if changes are made to the programs based on the visualization. 

**How did you choose which features of your chosen dataset to include or exclude from your visualization?**
I chose one specific table from the complete dataset to not overcomplicate and show a simple relationship. I chose to include all the years despite fall 2015 missing, since I think it is still usefull to see the full evolution of the other seasons.
